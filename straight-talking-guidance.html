No unnecessary upselling, ever

Most people can't verify a garage's advice — you're told what your car needs and you pay for it. Which is exactly why straight talking is the most repeated theme across more than a hundred Google reviews of this workshop: honest advice and no unnecessary upselling.

What straight talking looks like

  • What your car needs now, what can wait, and what doesn't need doing at all
  • Clear options so you can decide, not a single expensive path
  • Advice on whether a remap is even right for your car
  • A fault that needs watching rather than spending gets called that

Advice before invoices

Because we diagnose before quoting, our advice is built on findings — not on what would make the job bigger. The quote you get is a straightforward explanation and a clear price before anything is carried out. If the answer is 'nothing needs doing yet', that's the answer you'll get.

Talk it through first

If your car's doing something nobody's nailed down — or you've been quoted something that doesn't smell right — call the workshop on 01706 558 485. We'll tell you straight what we think, and whether it's worth booking in.
