Privacy Policy

Overview

DLR PERFORMANCE LTD ("DLR Performance", "we", "us") operates this website. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site and how we handle it. We are a UK-registered company (Company No. 11237435) and we handle personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

What we collect

  • Contact details you choose to share — such as your name, phone number and email address — when you get in touch through our contact form, by phone, or by email.
  • Details about your vehicle and the service you're asking about, where you provide them.
  • Basic technical information collected automatically, such as which pages were visited, through standard server logs or analytics.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, arrange appointments, and carry out work on your vehicle.
  • To communicate with you about bookings, work in progress, and collection.
  • To maintain records of work carried out, as required for accounting and tax purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with service providers (for example, payment processors or IT providers) where necessary to run the business, and where required by law.

The contact form

Our contact form uses a "mailto" link. When you submit it, the details you entered open in your own email application for you to send to us. The information is not stored on this website's server.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or erased, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you have concerns. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details on our contact page.

Cookies

This website does not set any advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy.
