Modern performance cars run on data — dozens of sensors reporting to the ECU every second. When one misreports, the symptoms can look like almost anything: rough running, a warning light, limp mode, or a fault that comes and goes. The expensive version of this story is a garage replacing parts one at a time until the symptom disappears. Our version is shorter: diagnose, confirm, then replace.

How replacement works here

  • Sensor diagnosis and replacement
  • Component verification before fitting — the failed part is confirmed, not guessed
  • Post-repair check to confirm the fix

Confirmed faults, not hunches

Replacing parts on a guess is how a sensor bill becomes a four-figure one. At DLR Performance the failed component is verified before anything is ordered or fitted, so you're paying for parts that were actually the problem. Cars often arrive here with a history of parts that didn't fix the issue — they leave with the cause confirmed and sorted.

Verified before it leaves

Fitting the part correctly is only half the job. Every replacement is checked after fitting and verified before the car leaves the workshop — the fix is confirmed, not assumed.
