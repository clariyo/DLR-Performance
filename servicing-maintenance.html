Servicing that keeps a performance car honest

Performance cars live harder and need a proper look-over, not a box-tick. Servicing at DLR Performance covers the checks that matter, uses quality parts, and comes with clear notes on what's fine, what to watch, and what's coming up.

Routine & specialist servicing
Quality, manufacturer-approved parts
Clear record of work carried out

A performance car driven the way it was designed to be driven places different demands on brakes, belts, tyres and fluids than an annual shopping run. Servicing at our Edenfield workshop isn't a box-ticking exercise — it covers the checks that matter for the car in front of us, with quality parts and a proper record of what was done.

What servicing covers

  • Routine & specialist servicing for performance and high-performance vehicles
  • Quality, manufacturer-approved parts
  • The checks that matter — not a generic checklist
  • A clear record of work carried out

Clear notes, not a mystery invoice

After every service you get a clear picture in plain terms: what's fine, what to watch, and what's coming up. It's the same honesty we bring to diagnostics — you shouldn't have to reverse-engineer an invoice to know what state your car is in.

Keeping a performance car honest

Customers stay with us because servicing here builds a history: what was replaced, what was flagged, and what we'll check next time. Over a few visits that history is the difference between a well-maintained performance car and an expensive surprise.
