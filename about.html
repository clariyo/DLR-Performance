A garage for people who actually like their car

DLR Performance is a specialist workshop in Edenfield, Bury, set up for performance and high-performance vehicles — and run by people who care how a car drives when it leaves the door.

The workshop
Who we are

Specialists, not generalists

DLR Performance is a registered limited company (DLR PERFORMANCE LTD, Company No. 11237435) and VAT-registered business based at Bridge Mills in Edenfield. We work on performance and high-performance vehicles — the kind of cars where diagnosis matters and a generic fix isn't good enough.

Customers come to us with the jobs other garages couldn't resolve, and they stay because the work is done properly, the advice is honest, and the car comes back right.

How we work

Principles we actually stick to

01

Diagnose before we quote

We find the real cause before recommending any work — so you're never paying for parts that weren't the problem.

02

Honest, clear advice

Transparent guidance on what your car needs now, what can wait, and what doesn't need doing at all. No upselling.

03

Quality parts, fitted right

Manufacturer-approved components, correct torque and setup, and a post-repair check to confirm the fix.
